StitchOps

One place to run a design studio: orders, invoices, customers, team and pay.

Dashboard

  • Revenue, orders and outstanding payments at a glance
  • Filter by date range and currency
  • Pending items flagged for quick action
  • Global search across orders, customers and invoices

Orders

  • Log orders with customer, designer, price and currency
  • Track status from pending to completed
  • Production cost and commission recorded per order
  • Filter by date, status, currency and search
  • Duplicate-submit protection on every save

Invoices

  • Automatic monthly invoicing of completed orders
  • Per-customer invoice numbering and prefixes
  • Add or remove orders on an invoice before sending
  • Mark paid with bank account and payment date
  • Overdue tracking and payment reminders

Customers

  • Customer profiles with codes, contacts and country
  • Free-trial vs paid customers, active / inactive
  • Assigned salesperson or company-owned
  • Full order and invoice history per customer
  • New-customer alerts for the admin

Team & Employees

  • Salespeople, designers and custom teams
  • Employee profiles with customers and earnings
  • Commission rates, base salary and payout day
  • Login accounts and role-based access
  • Assigned company email addresses

Payslips

  • Monthly payslip drafts built from earned commission and production
  • Edit included orders before issuing
  • Payslip history with numbered slips
  • Staff notified when slips are ready

Staff Portal

  • Salespeople see only their own customers
  • Personal payslips and earnings
  • Self-service profile and password change

Reports

  • Monthly report of sales, costs and profit
  • Breakdown by currency, customer and employee

Multi-currency

  • USD, GBP, AUD, EUR and a home currency
  • Exchange rates with live market updates
  • Totals converted to the default currency

Data Import & Export

  • Excel export of any list, matching current filters
  • Full backup export and one-click restore
  • Per-page import with column mapping
  • Import old order sheets: customers and staff matched or created automatically, with undo

Settings & Security

  • Company details, logo and bank accounts
  • SMTP email setup with connection test
  • Secure login, password reset requests
  • Admin-only areas and live notifications

Built for daily use

  • Fast server-side paging and search
  • Mobile-friendly layout and filter sheets
  • Real-time updates across devices
